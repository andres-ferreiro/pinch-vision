PinchVision

Your hands are the controls. Nine real-time looks, driven entirely by how you pinch.

The rack
Gestures
  • Pinch Index to thumb — tighter pinch, stronger effect
  • Tap Thumb to pinky — next effect for that hand
  • Rec Up to 20s, saved straight to your device
  • Blend One hand sweeps the rack, the other fades it
  • Window Touch both hands' fingertips — opens a window
  • Pin Hold thumb to pinky 1s — the window stays put
  • Lock Hold a fingertip on LOCK — freezes the look and the overlays
  • Draw Hold DRAW, then pinch to paint — brush and colour appear alongside
  • Photo Press index and middle together 0.7s — tap a frame to save
  • Reset Close both fists 1s — drops every window, leaves blend mode

Nothing leaves your device — the camera feed and the tracking both run locally.

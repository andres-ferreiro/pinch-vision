Pinch Vision

Your hands are the controls. Eight real-time looks, driven entirely by how you pinch.

  • Pinch index to thumb — tighter pinch, stronger effect
  • Tap thumb to pinky — next effect for that hand
  • Record up to 20s and save it to your device
  • Blend one hand sweeps the rack slowly, the other fades it

Nothing leaves your device — the camera feed and the tracking both run locally.
